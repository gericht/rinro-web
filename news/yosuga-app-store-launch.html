Yosuga is now available on the App Store

2026-07-25

Yosuga, the iOS app that turns saving into a promise to your future self, is now available on the App Store. Entrust something you care about, and a quiet word arrives on the promised day so you can see it through — reading, watching, going, buying, or making. The promise loop (entrust → word → today's promises → fulfill) is free to use.

See the product page for the full details. We'll keep refining the app so it can hand your promises to your future self, quietly and surely.

App Store: https://apps.apple.com/app/id6779824639
