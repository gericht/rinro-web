THE PROMISE LOOP

Save it, and forget.
When the day comes, a quiet word.

Yosuga isn't a box for hoarding information. It turns the promises you've entrusted into something you can keep, and hands them back to your future self, for certain.

01 — Entrust

Entrust it, from Share.

Hand one URL to your future self from the iOS share sheet. The moment asks for almost nothing — just choose how you'll meet it again. The title and thumbnail are filled in for you, and you can settle on the promised day later, in calm.

Read Watch Go Buy Make
02 — Word

That day, a word arrives.

When the promised day comes, we let you know, quietly. “You were going to watch this.” Fulfill, postpone, or let go — the choice is yours. For things you keep postponing, we gently add: “Shall we let this go?”

03 — Today's promises

Find today's promises.

The home screen holds no long lists. It quietly shows only what has come to meet you today. A promise drawing near glows as a warm golden star. On an empty day, it simply says, “No promises today.”

04 — Fulfill

Fulfill the promise.

Read, watch, go, buy, make — only by seeing it through does it become worth something. Each time you fulfill one, the sky grows a little brighter.

05 — In Keeping

Go and meet what you've entrusted.

Beyond waiting for word, you can go to everything you've entrusted whenever you wish. They're ordered by the time of each promise. Filter by how you'll meet it, and meet it on the spot — even before the promised day.

06 — Waiting promises

Notice the promises that wait.

Even past the promised day, there's nothing to worry about. In the coral of dusk, it just lets you know, quietly — so the small guilt of not having looked never piles up.

MEET IT, DEEPLY — Pro

When the day comes, see it through, properly.

A quiet set of tools to carry each promise to the end. The promise loop is always free. Only the depth and the comfort come with the one-time Pro.

07 — Reader viewPRO

Your half-read, anywhere.

A quiet reading view with just the article text. Keep the text in hand, so you can pick up where you left off even with no signal.

08 — ListenPRO

Listen, and fulfill.

It reads the article aloud, so you can take it in by ear — on a walk, between chores. Even when your hands are full, you can keep your promise.

09 — ReflectionPRO

Fill up, looking back.

This week's count fulfilled, your completion rate, average days to fulfill, a breakdown by how you'll meet it. The promises you've kept quietly add up. We don't measure by how much you saved — what matters is whether you saw it through.

10 — Widget

Today's promises, on your Home Screen.

Even with notifications off, you're fine. A small window on your Home Screen shows today's promises at a glance, quietly.
