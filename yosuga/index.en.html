02 · Date

Just pick a day.

Today, tomorrow, the weekend. It shows up in that day’s Up Next. You can also decide later; anything without a date waits in its own section.

  • Dates are concrete: “Sat, Oct 10”, not just “tomorrow”
  • Read and Watch go up to 2 weeks out; Visit and Buy up to 3 months

03 · Notify

One notification, on that day.

You get one notification on days with something scheduled. It names the item, so you know what’s up without opening the app. It starts at 9:00; change it anytime in Settings.

  • Notifications off? The widget still shows today’s items
  • Nothing scheduled, nothing sent

04 · Done

Read it, and it’s done.

Open it in the app, then tap Read. There’s no confirmation dialog; if you slip, just undo. When today’s list is empty, it says so and shows the next date.

  • The button matches the type: Read, Watched, Visited, Bought, Made
  • Done items clear automatically after 30 days
